## Phase Goal

Operate the microproduct as an outcome-focused product, not just a shipped codebase.

## What Must Be True After Launch

- The product has a stable public URL and a repeatable, reversible release workflow. See [Microproduct Deployment](/docs/playbook/operate/microproduct-deployment).
- You know who the product serves and how they find it.
- You capture behavioral signals that inform what to improve next.
- You run a repeatable cycle for hypothesis, build, measure, and decide.

## Required Outputs

- First distribution loops tied to a specific audience.
- Product learning backlog with evidence and confidence levels.
- Iteration plan based on real usage, not assumptions.

## Marketing Ownership

- Define the first distribution loops tied to a specific audience.
- Ship simple messaging that communicates problem, value, and proof.
- Track channel performance and double down on what converts.

## Insights Ownership

- Capture behavioral signals across activation, usage, and retention.
- Maintain a product learning backlog with evidence and confidence levels.
- Run recurring insight reviews to guide roadmap decisions.

## Iteration Ownership

Use a repeatable cycle:

1. Hypothesis
2. Build
3. Measure
4. Decide

## Operating Rhythm After Launch

- **Weekly**: review metrics, user feedback, and top failures.
- **Biweekly**: prioritize experiments and scope the next iteration.
- **Monthly**: reassess positioning, market assumptions, and unit economics.
