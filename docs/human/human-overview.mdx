## Layout Guide

You can read the playbook like a textbook. Move through it sequentially, clicking
each next page in order, and the structure carries you from opening concepts into
later chapters, one layer at a time.

That linear path is useful, but it is not the only way to read. Some readers come
looking for a specific module, topic, or author. Others have already read the full
sequence and only want the newest or most relevant parts as the playbook grows.

For those readers, the tree becomes a web. Modules point across branches to
related or complementary material that may not appear next in the sequential
path. The tree gives the playbook its structure; the web gives it movement,
letting you follow the knowledge you need instead of retracing every step.

If you are automating builds with AI agents, see the [agents hub](/agents) for
machine-readable entrypoints. The human playbook remains the canonical source for
concepts and decisions.
